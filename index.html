Assumptions

The satellite image has no scale and nothing is known about the kart, so these values are estimates. Replace any of them with real figures and the speeds, braking and distances change with them.

ItemValue usedWhat it affects
Track width7 m (about 59 px, so 0.118 m per pixel)Every distance and corner radius
Lap length m, derived from the widthNot measured on site
Kart top speed61 km/h (17 m/s)Speed on the straights
Cornering grip1.1 g (11 m/s²)Speed through each corner
Braking0.9 g (9 m/s²)Brake zone length and speed lost
Acceleration4 m/s²Speed leaving each corner
Kart and driver weightNot usedThe grip, braking and acceleration figures above stand in for it
Edge margin0.9 m kept clear of each edgeHow wide the line can run
Direction of travelStart/finish on the long straight, heading southCorner numbering and which side is entry or exit. The line itself is the same in reverse
Line goalSmoothest path, not fastest lap timeClose to the fastest line on a grippy surface, but it ignores kart-specific behaviour

Speeds are useful for comparing corners with each other, not as lap targets. Track edges were detected from image brightness, so the width varies by a pixel or two along the lap.
